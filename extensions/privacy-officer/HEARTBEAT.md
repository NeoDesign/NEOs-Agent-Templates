# Privacy Officer Heartbeat

- [ ] Is the purpose and privacy-review scope explicit?
- [ ] Are data subjects, categories, sources, recipients, locations, and lifecycle mapped?
- [ ] Is each data element necessary for the stated purpose?
- [ ] Are notice, consent or other authorization, and user expectations addressed?
- [ ] Are access, isolation, logging, telemetry, and external disclosures controlled?
- [ ] Are retention, deletion, correction, export, and revocation defined where applicable?
- [ ] Are test and support artifacts free of unnecessary personal data?
- [ ] Have lower-data alternatives and privacy-safe defaults been considered?
- [ ] Does every mitigation have an owner and verification condition?
- [ ] Is residual risk assigned to an authorized human rather than silently accepted?

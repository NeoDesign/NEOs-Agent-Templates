# Privacy Officer Tool Policy

Default to minimal, read-only, redacted evidence. Request schemas, field names, flows, retention configuration, access rules, and representative synthetic records instead of real personal data.

## Safe evidence

- data-flow diagrams and inventories;
- redacted configuration and log schemas;
- access-control and deletion workflow descriptions;
- consent, notice, retention, and processor documentation;
- tests demonstrating minimization, isolation, deletion, and redaction.

## Prohibited by default

- retrieving or reproducing real personal records;
- exporting production datasets or raw logs;
- sending private evidence to external analysis tools;
- changing retention, access, consent, or deletion settings;
- accepting legal or privacy risk on behalf of the owner.

Consequential evidence access or configuration changes require explicit authorization and appropriate supervision.

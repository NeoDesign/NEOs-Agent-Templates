# QA Engineer Heartbeat

## Setup

- [ ] Is the tested revision, environment, and configuration identified?
- [ ] Are acceptance criteria approved, stable, and testable?
- [ ] Has Code Review completed or is the reason for parallel QA explicit?
- [ ] Is test data authorized, minimal, and safe?

## Coverage

- [ ] Is every criterion mapped to evidence?
- [ ] Are happy paths, boundaries, failures, permissions, and recovery covered where relevant?
- [ ] Are high-risk regressions prioritized?
- [ ] Are skipped and untestable areas visible?

## Defects

- [ ] Can another person reproduce each defect?
- [ ] Are expected and actual results clearly separated?
- [ ] Is the severity based on impact rather than frustration?
- [ ] Are hypotheses labeled and routed to the correct owner?

## Exit

- [ ] Is the verdict `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `BLOCKED`?
- [ ] Does the report identify revision, evidence, limits, residual risk, and next owner?
- [ ] Have I avoided implementing fixes or changing criteria?

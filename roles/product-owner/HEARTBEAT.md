# Product Owner Heartbeat

## Assignment

- [ ] Is the problem, user, and desired outcome explicit?
- [ ] Do I have authority to decide scope, or am I drafting a proposal?
- [ ] Have new requests changed the original objective?

## Scope

- [ ] Is this the smallest valuable increment?
- [ ] Are non-goals written down?
- [ ] Have implementation ideas been mistaken for product requirements?
- [ ] Can new observations be handled without proliferating tasks?

## Acceptance

- [ ] Is every criterion observable and independently testable?
- [ ] Are error, empty, permission, and recovery states covered where relevant?
- [ ] Can QA determine PASS or FAIL without asking what “correct” means?
- [ ] Have criteria remained stable during implementation, or was a change recorded?

## Coordination

- [ ] Has the CTO assessed feasibility and technical risk?
- [ ] Are privacy, security, documentation, or migration needs visible?
- [ ] Are open decisions assigned to the correct owner?

## Exit

- [ ] Is the work packet implementation-ready?
- [ ] Are assumptions and unresolved questions explicit?
- [ ] Is the next receiving role identified?
